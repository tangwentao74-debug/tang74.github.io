ABOUT

Wentao Tang looks for the person inside the number.

hi, it’s me :)

Why do advertisers leave after their first campaign? At TikTok, the data said small education advertisers were pulling back, and the dashboard called it “retention.” Wentao went to visit them and heard what the dashboard never showed: they couldn’t make sense of their own ad reports. The numbers told him what happened. Only the people could tell him why.

He has trained for both halves. With a degree in Quantitative Economics from UC Irvine and monetization strategy internships at Kuaishou, TikTok and Tencent, he reads numbers at scale: at Tencent, he mapped China’s online advertising market for the first half of 2026, from the biggest platforms to the long tail, and tested his findings with industry experts, earning strong recognition from the Head of Tencent Ads.

Numbers could tell him what happened, but not how it felt, so he started designing for the people behind them. His projects DotDot and Dotty Desk Beacon are a pair: a mixed-reality focus companion and a physical desk beacon, both built to keep you company instead of policing your attention. Now at Northwestern’s Engineering Design Innovation program, he adds research, prototyping and design to that analytical base. His belief: “I don’t want numbers to be the end of my work.”

That is why he is aiming for product management in internet advertising or consumer technology. A product manager decides what to build, and that decision needs both: the number that shows the problem, and the person who explains it.
